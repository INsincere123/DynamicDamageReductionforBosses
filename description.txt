装备太强，Boss 还没反应就倒了？

这个 Mod 能让你重新好好享受一场 Boss 战——
无论装备多强，Boss 都会撑够你设定的时间，让战斗完整地打完。
血量下降曲线平滑自然，不会卡住，也不会打出满屏的 1。

同时也是模组开发者和武器测试者的得力工具——
高 DPS 武器无法在前期 Boss 身上正常测试？强制 Boss 存活足够时间，让你完整收集战斗数据。

功能：
· 为每个 Boss 单独设置最短存活时间
· 动态减伤，战斗过程自然流畅
· 无需物品，直接在 Mod 配置界面操作
· 支持灾厄 Mod、Fargo's Souls Mod、瑟银 Mod

Geared up so well that bosses drop before the fight even starts?

This mod lets you actually enjoy the battle —
no matter how strong your gear, the boss will last as long as you set it to.
HP drains smoothly and naturally; no sudden stalling, no walls of 1s.

Also a handy tool for mod developers and weapon testers —
can't properly test high-DPS weapons on early bosses? Force them to survive long enough to gather real combat data.

Features:
· Set a minimum survival time for each boss individually
· Dynamic damage reduction that feels natural in-game
· No items required — configure everything in the Mod Config UI
· Supports Calamity Mod, Fargo's Souls Mod, and Thorium Mod
